Showcase master catalog

Root hub of complete interfaces built with Matios UI components. Each screen opens in a modal with a live preview and copyable code.

Mode
Accent

Showcase groups

For now only Authentication, Onboarding, Users, Profiles, Roles, Workflows and Security are visible.

Authentication

Initial showcase family. It contains the unified login ready to use and the rest of the flow as controlled placeholders.

Onboarding

Start of the signup and setup flow. The group begins with signup variants for single-company and multi-company products.

Users

Operational management of internal accounts: list, create, edit, detail and password change from a simple administrative surface.

Profiles

Per-module profiles with fine-grained checkable permissions. They serve as the basis for roles to later group one profile per module.

Roles

Roles as a grouping of per-module profiles. Each module allows a single profile selected within the role.

Support

Base flows for operational helpdesk: triage inbox, case detail and the next states of the support cycle.

Productivity

Flows to manage work and time: task board, weekly planning and the team's next operational views.

Documents

Lightweight views for files and operational content. The focus is to provide copyable experiences that do not depend on dense tables or complex document managers.

Search

Cross-cutting search, refinement and discovery for products that need to find people, content and settings without relying on isolated views.

Account

Screens for personal profile, security and account preferences. The focus is to provide copyable views so a real product can start fast without inventing new UI.

Settings

Workspace settings and shared policies. The goal is to provide reusable views for real products that need configuration without relying on tables or dashboards.

Admin

Operational management of members, invitations and initial workspace access. The focus is to provide real views for B2B products and internal platforms.

Billing

Plan, charges and payment methods for SaaS products. These are screens ready to copy into real contexts without relying on dashboards.

Integrations

Catalog and configuration of external connections for B2B products. These are useful views to catalog providers, sync status and support actions without resorting to mockups.

Security

Sessions, auditing and traceability for products that need real visibility into access, sensitive activity and operational control.

Developers

Tools for integrators and technical teams: credentials, access control and touch points with APIs or automations.

Workflows

Inboxes, tasks and workflow definitions for permissions, documents, budgets and any other requirement that needs stages, routing and traceability.

System

Empty states, maintenance and operational errors ready to copy into real products. These are base screens that usually get left for last and are already solved here.

Notifications

Alert center, recent activity and cross-cutting product tracking. The focus is to provide copyable patterns for notices and feeds without relying on tables.
